Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Serial not recorded · Manufactured not recorded

Reported symptom: AC not cooling upstairs

Matched documentation for: Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Try this first

  1. Check that the thermostat is set to cool mode

    Before calling a dealer, make sure your thermostat is set to cool. [4]

  2. Inspect and clean or replace the indoor air filter

    A dirty or clogged filter can block airflow and prevent cooling. [2]

  3. Check the outdoor unit for debris and blockage

    Clear any leaves, dirt, or objects blocking the outdoor coil. [2]

  4. Verify the condensate drain line is not clogged

    The safety float switch shuts off cooling when the condensate drain line backs up. Most Trane central AC systems have this safety feature. [5]
