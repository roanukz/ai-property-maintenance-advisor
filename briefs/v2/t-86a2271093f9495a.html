Service brief

Sundance Spas Optima 880

Serial 100915742 · Manufactured 06/2014

Reported symptom: not heating

Matched documentation for: Sundance Spas Optima 880

Try this first

  1. SAFETY: Run the spa for a few minutes without the filter installed

    If heating resumes, the filter is the culprit and should be cleaned or replaced [2]

  2. Check water level is at the correct fill line

    An improper water level can cause various operational problems including heating issues [2]

  3. Remove and inspect the spa's filter for debris, clogs, or trapped air

    Carefully remove the filter and check for visible blockages [2]
