Service brief

Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Serial not recorded · Manufactured not recorded

Reported symptom: outdoor unit runs but the fan does not spin

Matched documentation for: Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Try this first

  1. SAFETY: Check the circuit breaker box for the breaker labeled for the air conditioner unit.

    If the breaker is tripped, switch it off and then back on. [1]

  2. Visually inspect the outdoor unit fan blades for damage, obstruction, or debris.

    Ensure the blades are not bent, cracked, or blocked by leaves, ice, or other material. [2]
