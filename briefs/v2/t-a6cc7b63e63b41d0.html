Service brief

Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Serial not recorded · Manufactured not recorded

Reported symptom: outdoor unit runs but the fan does not spin

Matched documentation for: Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Try this first

  1. SAFETY: Check the circuit breaker labeled for the outdoor unit

    If tripped, this is a power supply issue; reset it if safe to do so [1]

  2. Visually inspect the outdoor fan blades for damage or obstruction

    Look for broken blades, debris, or anything preventing rotation [2]

  3. Keep the outdoor unit clear of debris and vegetation

    Remove any blockages around the unit to maximize airflow [1]
