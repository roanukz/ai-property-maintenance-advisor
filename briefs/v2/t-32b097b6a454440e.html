Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Serial not recorded · Manufactured not recorded

Reported symptom: AC not cooling upstairs

Matched documentation for: Trane XR16 (4TTR6036)

Try this first

  1. Check that the thermostat is set to COOL and the fan is set to AUTO.

    If someone got cold, they may have changed the thermostat settings without you realizing it. [3]

  2. Make sure the thermostat's set temperature is lower than the current indoor temperature.

    [3]

  3. Examine and replace the air filters if they are clogged or dirty.

    Consult the owner's guide to find the exact location of the air filters in the system. [2]

  4. Try turning the thermostat off for 1-3 hours to see if the evaporator coil will defrost.

    Also check for signs of water leakage below the indoor unit. [2]
