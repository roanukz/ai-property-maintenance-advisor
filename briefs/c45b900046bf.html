Try this first

  1. SAFETY: Kill power before touching filters or any panel

    Trane's own troubleshooting guidance for an AC that is not cooling: "Set your AC to 'OFF' and turn off the breakers that supply it with power" before cleaning or replacing filters. Do this before opening any filter door or approaching the outdoor unit. [1]

  2. Confirm the thermostat is in cooling mode and set below room temperature

    Trane: check the thermostat setting and make sure it is set to cool; if it is set to cool, confirm the temperature setting is at the desired temperature. If it is not set to cool, switch to cooling operation. [1]

  3. Check for a schedule or energy-saving mode that is delaying cooling

    Documented check: confirm that no one changed the schedule or turned on energy-saving modes that delay cooling. Relevant at a rental where guests or a prior cleaner may have altered settings. [2]

  4. Replace dead thermostat batteries

    Documented remedy for a system that will not run/cool: turn on the thermostat or replace the batteries. [3]

  5. Clean or replace the air filter

    With power off at the breaker, clean or replace the filters. Consult a local Trane Comfort Specialist if you are unsure where the filters are located. [1]

  6. SAFETY: Clear debris from the outdoor condenser coil

    Documented remedy when the outdoor cooling coil is clogged or blocked: clean up the debris in the outside cooling coil. Keep power off at the breaker while doing this. [3]

  7. SAFETY: Check whether the circuit breaker is tripped

    Documented remedy: reset the circuit breaker. If it trips again after reset, stop and leave it off for a technician. [3]
